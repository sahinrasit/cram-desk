Privacy Policy

CramDesk · Last updated 12 October 2026

SupportTürkçe

Short version: CramDesk has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your PDFs and flashcards stay on your device and in your own iCloud. We do not collect, sell or share your study data.

What stays on your device

  • PDFs, flashcards, review history and exam dates are stored on your iPhone or iPad. If you are signed in to iCloud, they sync through your own private iCloud database, which we cannot access.
  • Card generation runs on your device with Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence). Page text is never sent to us or to any server.
  • Text recognition of scanned pages happens on your device with Apple's Vision framework.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. To validate purchases and understand sales, the app shares purchase information (an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID, the product purchased, price, currency, country/storefront and transaction dates) with RevenueCat, Inc. This information is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications — only if you turn on the daily review reminder.
  • Files — CramDesk only opens the PDFs you choose to import.

You can change these at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Children

CramDesk is a study tool for students and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Contact

Questions about privacy: mrsahinrasit@gmail.com. If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
